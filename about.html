Our story

Two Decades of Exceptional Care

HMRT Polyclinic opened its doors in January 2006 with one commitment: to deliver dental, medical and surgical care to the highest standards of excellence. Today, part of the Dr Yarub Group, we have served thousands of patients from across Dubai and the UAE.

Our team — led by oral & maxillofacial surgeon Dr. Yarub Al Shammary — combines state-of-the-art technology with a gentle, personal approach. Patients tell us what they value most: professional doctors, kind staff and clear explanations at every step.

  • Specialist-led surgical & implant care
  • On-site imaging for accurate diagnosis
  • Direct billing with major UAE insurers
  • Arabic, English & translation services
