## 9. Design direction

- **Mood:** premium, calm and clinical, with a "royal" feel that suits the name.
- **Palette (suggested):** deep navy `#0F2A44` or emerald `#0E4D45`, gold accent `#C9A24A`, ivory background `#FAF7F0`, ink `#1A1A1A`.
- **Type:** Latin headings in *Playfair Display* or *Cormorant* with body in *Inter*. Arabic in *Tajawal* or *Cairo* (body) and *Amiri* or *Noto Kufi Arabic* (headings).
- **Bilingual:** use `dir="rtl"` on the Arabic pages and mirror the layout with CSS logical properties. Phone numbers stay left-to-right (wrap them in `<bdi dir="ltr">`). Put a language toggle in the header.
- **Imagery:** real photos of the clinic, team and treatment rooms. The client must supply high-resolution originals. Google and Facebook thumbnails are too small and the clinic's ownership of them is unclear.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG AA contrast, especially for gold text on light backgrounds.
