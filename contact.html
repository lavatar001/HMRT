FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. We work with Daman, ADNIC, MetLife, NextCare, Mednet, Neuron, Almadallah, Saada and Enaya. Contact us with your card details and we'll confirm your coverage.

Where are you located, and is there parking?

We are on the 11th floor of Mai Tower (Offices 1107–1108), Amman Street, Al Nahda 1, Dubai. Free parking is available nearby, along with RTA parking and valet.

Are you open on Fridays?

The clinic is closed on Fridays. We are open Saturday to Thursday, 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM.

Do you treat children?

Yes — we welcome children and offer paediatric dentistry in a calm, friendly setting.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call us on +971 4 265 5888 or message us on WhatsApp during opening hours and we'll arrange the earliest available appointment.
